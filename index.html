About Us

GSM Wizard Agent Banking Tech (also known as GSM Wizard Last-Mile Payments Co) is a forward-thinking fintech company dedicated to expanding financial inclusion across Nigeria. Based in Abeokuta, Ogun State, we specialize in agent banking services and last-mile payment solutions that bring essential financial services to underserved communities.

Led by experienced professionals in crypto operations and digital finance, we combine cutting-edge technology with deep local market knowledge to create payment infrastructure that works for every Nigerian. Our mission is simple: to ensure that no one is left behind in Nigeria's digital economy transformation.

Our Services

Agent Banking Solutions

We deploy and manage agent banking networks that bring banking services to communities without traditional bank branches. Our agents provide cash deposits, withdrawals, bill payments, and account opening services.

Last-Mile Payment Infrastructure

Bridging the gap between financial institutions and end-users through innovative payment technology, ensuring transactions reach even the most remote locations.

Digital Transaction Processing

Secure, fast, and reliable processing of digital payments including mobile money transfers, airtime purchases, and utility bill payments.

Crypto Operations & Integration

Expert guidance and infrastructure for businesses looking to incorporate cryptocurrency and blockchain solutions into their payment ecosystems.

Agent Network Management

Comprehensive training, monitoring, and support for agent banking networks to ensure service quality and compliance.

Financial Inclusion Consulting

Strategic advisory services for organizations seeking to expand their reach into underserved markets across Nigeria.
