About Us

GSM Wizard Communication Limited is a leading manufacturing company strategically located in Nnewi, the industrial hub of Anambra State. We specialize in the production and assembly of high-quality communication equipment, devices, and accessories that meet international standards.

Our facility combines cutting-edge technology with skilled Nigerian craftsmanship to deliver products that drive digital connectivity across West Africa. With a commitment to innovation, quality control, and customer satisfaction, we have established ourselves as a trusted name in the communication manufacturing sector.

Our team of experienced engineers and technicians work tirelessly to ensure every product leaving our factory meets the highest standards of performance and durability.

Communication Equipment Assembly

Our facility handles complete assembly of communication hardware, from circuit board integration to final product testing and packaging.
